# Real-source notes

The source strategy is based on publicly visible commercial lead mechanisms, not fabricated records.

- EC21 publicly documents a Buying Leads mechanism and its public homepage currently exposes new buying requests. The platform also states that posted buying leads are reviewed before publication.
- MATRADE publicly describes trade enquiries from foreign buyers being disseminated as Trade Leads and its business matching programmes with international buyers.
- TradeIndia publicly exposes Buy Trade Leads pages.
- Go4WorldBusiness publicly documents buyer-lead workflows and matched buy-lead functionality.

These facts establish that the sources contain genuine commercial-signal categories. They do **not** by themselves grant GiMtradefin permission to automate collection. That is why Stage 1 separates `registered` from `enabled + permission_confirmed`.
